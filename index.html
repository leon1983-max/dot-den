The Dot Den is a personal games page — built to collect the small, sharp, two-minute games worth coming back to. Five are live so far: the boxes-and-lines classic Dots and Boxes, its stranger cousin Dots, where you fence in the other player's own dots, the drop-and-line classic Connect Four, the flanking-capture classic Reversi, and a cube-sized 3D Tic-Tac-Toe. All five play hot-seat, against the computer, or online against someone in another location.
