The Dot Den is a personal games page — built to collect the small, sharp, two-minute games worth coming back to. Two are live so far: the boxes-and-lines classic Dots and Boxes, and its stranger cousin Dots, where you fence in the other player's own dots. Both play hot-seat, against the computer, or online against someone in another location.
